#def 
- (*def.*) **modifiers** are keywords added to variable, method, or class declarations to change their behavior, visibility, or scope.
- **modifiers** are usually divided into two main categories:
	- **Access Modifiers**
		- define the *visibility* or *scope* of a method
		- (*i.o.w.*) determine which other classes are allowed to call that method.
	- **Non-Access Modifiers**
		- do not change the visibility, but change the **functional properties** of the method.
