#java #logic
- Logical operators allow you to connect and modify [[boolean]] expressions.
- **Operators**:
    - [[logical NOT]] (`!`) - Returns the opposite of the current value.
    - [[logical AND]] (`&&`) - Returns `true` only if both sides of the expression are `true`.
    - [[logical OR]] (`||`) - Returns `true` if at least one side of the expression is `true`.
