#java #object-oriented-programming
- [[Instance]] - a specific version of a [[Class]]; "instance" and "object" generally refer to the same thing.
- **Naming Convention**:
    - [[Class]] names are usually capitalized (e.g., `Student`).
    - [[Object]]/[[Instance]] names are usually lowercase (e.g., `ada`).
- Relationship: "An [[Object]] is an [[Instance]] of a [[Class]]."
