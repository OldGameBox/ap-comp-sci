 #java
- **Methods** can be improved by adding **parameters** to make them more general-purpose.
- **[[parameters|Parameters]]** - inputs to a method that allow it to perform tasks with varying data.
- **[[Calling a method]]** with parameters: You must provide the specific values (arguments) that the method expects.
- Examples from video:
    - `printLineBreak(int length)` - uses a [[for loop]] to print a specific number of characters.
    - `sum(int a, int b)` - takes two integers and prints their total using [[System.out.println()]].
